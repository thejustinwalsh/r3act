Neither source deletion counts nor a warm benchmark closes those requirements.

## Completed-boundary rule

A slice is finished only after implementation, removal of its superseded production mechanism, primary and independent integration review, consolidated correctness/package/host/HMR verification and the declared bounded performance episode. Failed attempts remain visible. Unfinished requirements retain explicit status. Current source is uncommitted/unpushed while this boundary is open.
