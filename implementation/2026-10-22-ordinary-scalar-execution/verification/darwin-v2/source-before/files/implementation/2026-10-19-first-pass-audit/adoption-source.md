That AI-authored host proves feasibility, not human comprehension or maintenance advantage.

Pure React Refresh retention, native reload/remount, old pending-work cancellation and final teardown are the still-unrun installed browser contract (`implementation/2026-10-19-installed-hmr/design.md:1-9`; `results.md:1-3,31-37`).
